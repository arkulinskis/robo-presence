Run it in a browser

This robot's brain is a phone or laptop. Open robot mode on that device (signed in as you), point the camera ahead and press Start. Keep the screen on.

Upload the Arduino bridge

In the Arduino IDE: new sketch, paste this (its CONFIG block matches your Parts tab), pick your board and port, Upload. Then plug the Arduino into the Pi's USB. The Pi asks it what it has when it starts.

Shopping list

  • Raspberry Pi 4 or 5 (a Zero 2 W works at 320 × 240)
  • Camera Module 3, or any USB webcam
  • A 2WD/4WD chassis or tank tracks with two motors
  • Motor driver: TB6612FNG (recommended), L298N or DRV8833
  • Two SG90/MG90S servos + a pan-tilt bracket
  • Battery pack (2S Li-ion, 7.4 V) + a 5 V 3 A step-down for the Pi
  • Optional: Arduino Nano/Uno, HC-SR04 sonar, LEDs, a buzzer, a small speaker

How the connection works

The robot checks in with Robo Presence every second or two. When you press Drive, your browser leaves a connection offer, the robot answers, and from then on video and controls go directly between you (WebRTC, the same tech as video calls), typically 50 to 200 ms. If a network blocks direct connections, an admin can add a TURN relay in Admin → Network.
