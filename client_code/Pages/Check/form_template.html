Connection check

Will driving work from this network? Ten seconds of tests from this browser: WebRTC, the server, your router's NAT, and the relay. Nothing is recorded.

What the results mean

Direct (P2P): video and controls go straight between your browser and the robot. Fastest.

Strict network: some routers, phone networks (4G/5G), offices and hotels hand out a new port for every server. Two strict networks can't meet directly, so drives go through a relay (a TURN server): it works, with a little more delay.

Both ends count: the robot's Wi-Fi matters as much as yours. If either side is easy, a direct connection usually works.
